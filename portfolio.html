About

A little more context

I'm a Computer Engineering graduate from Laguna State Polytechnic University — San Pablo City Campus, with hands-on experience across system development and hardware-software integration. I like projects where the software has to talk to something physical: a sensor, a microcontroller, a recognition model that needs to respond in real time.

My thesis paired game design with accessibility — an arcade-style system that teaches Filipino Sign Language to children with hearing and speech impairments, built around a real-time recognition pipeline. It placed 2nd for Best Paper (Experimental/Developmental Study) at the 2026 College of Engineering Students Research Congress.
